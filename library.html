Grass-Fed Tallow

INCI: BOVINE TALLOW

›

Grass-Fed Tallow

WHAT IT IS

A rendered animal fat traditionally used as a source of rich, stable lipids.

WHY WE USE IT

It gives the balm dense emollience and a protective lipid feel on skin.

WHAT IT CONTRIBUTES

Richness, cushion, lipid nourishment and an occlusive skin feel.

WHAT IT DOESN’T DO

It is not a miracle ingredient. It is a lipid with a defined formulation role.

Pure Cera Alba

INCI: CERA ALBA (BEESWAX)

›

Pure Cera Alba

WHAT IT IS

Cera Alba is a natural wax produced by honeybees.

WHY WE USE IT

Without structure, a balm becomes an oil. Beeswax provides architecture.

WHAT IT CONTRIBUTES

Firmness, consistency, viscosity and a protective film-forming character.

WHAT IT DOESN’T DO

It does not repair skin. Its role is to give the balm physical form.

A powdered starch obtained from plants such as Maranta arundinacea.
